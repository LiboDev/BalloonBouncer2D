# Balloon Bouncer — promotional copy

## Store listing

**Title:** Balloon Bouncer  
**Genre:** Arcade / physics puzzle  
**Tagline:** Fling once. Watch everything pop.

**Short description**  
Aim, fling, and ricochet through colorful balloon fields. Pop enough to clear each level, earn coins, and build a stronger launch.

**Full description**  
Line up your shot, pull back, and let your balls bounce through a field of balloons and rails. Every pop earns coins. Clear at least 75% of the balloons to move on, then spend your winnings on saw blades, fireballs, lightning orbs, and black holes. New balloon types raise the challenge as you progress: fire balloons explode, ice and stone balloons scatter shards, and armored balloons take more hits. Chase a perfect clear, tune your next launch, and watch one ricochet become a chain reaction.

**Three feature callouts**

1. **Fling and ricochet:** Pull back to set your angle and power, then watch every ball in your loadout bounce through the level.
2. **Pop for upgrades:** Earn coins from balloons and add stronger ball types between levels.
3. **Clear the field:** Beat the 75% target or aim for a perfect clear as new balloon types appear.

**Controls**  
Touch or mouse: drag back on the game area and release to launch. Keyboard: arrow keys adjust aim and power; Space or Enter launches.
